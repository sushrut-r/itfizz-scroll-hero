How it works

Three weeks, no new hardware.

ITFIZZ sits on top of the tools your kitchens already run. Nothing gets replaced, and nothing gets ripped out on a Friday night.

01

Ingest

Connect the POS, courier webhooks and call logs. First board is live the same afternoon — no data team required.

02

Sequence

Every order takes a slot on the live board. Whatever is at risk floats to the top before a customer notices.

03

Prove

Four numbers, one weekly delta, exportable before the finance review. If a kitchen slips, you see it that week.
